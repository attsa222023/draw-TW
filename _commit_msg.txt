Auto-avoid overlapping labels on the review map

drawReviewMarkers() always placed each label at a fixed offset from
its own dot, so two nearby answers (common in the special-mode meme
pool especially) could end up with overlapping name pills. Now does a
two-pass layout: compute where every label naturally wants to sit,
then run a small iterative pairwise-separation pass
(resolveLabelCollisions()) that nudges any two overlapping label boxes
apart along whichever axis needs the smaller push, clamped to stay
on-canvas. A label nudged more than a few pixels off its natural spot
gets a thin leader line back to its dot, so it's still obviously whose
label is whose once several are jumbled together.

Shared by both the on-screen review canvas and the share-card image
(buildScoreCard() reuses the same function), so both get the fix.
